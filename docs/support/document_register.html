Title Document Register
Doc# DOC-COMP-006
Version 1.0
Date 18-09-2023

The document register serves as a comprehensive and systematic catalog of documents related to Tessera operations. Its primary purpose is to maintain a record of all documents, including their current status, location, and version, ensuring that stakeholders can quickly access accurate and up-to-date information. This register helps in enforcing document control practices, enhancing transparency, and facilitating effective communication among all parties involved.
