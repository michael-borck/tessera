This is the baseline financial projection for Tessera across the planning
period, showing projected revenue, expenses and net income by month. It is a
planning artefact intended to support trend analysis and budget conversations,
not a committed forecast: the figures should be read alongside the operational
realities of the period, including the first-year impact of the
TSR-INC-2025-031 breach (estimated at ~$1.8M, with 23 tenants not renewing),
which is not yet reflected in the baseline line items below.

| Month | Projected revenue | Projected expenses | Net income |

| 2024-04-30 | 12000 | 7000 | 5000 |
| 2024-05-31 | 15000 | 8000 | 7000 |
| 2024-06-30 | 18000 | 7500 | 10500 |
| 2024-07-31 | 20000 | 7800 | 12200 |
| 2024-08-31 | 22000 | 8200 | 13800 |

> **REVIEWER NOTE:** The magnitudes in the baseline table are not reconciled to
   a business of Tessera's scale (~210 staff, 600+ clients). Treat the figures
   as an illustrative planning baseline tied to the source dataset rather than
   a management account, and do not quote them as the company's financial
   position.
